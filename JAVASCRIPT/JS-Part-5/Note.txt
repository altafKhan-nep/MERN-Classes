Random Integer:
From 1 to 10:

step 1: let num = Math.random()

step 2: num = num * 10

step 3: num = Math.floor(num)

step 4: num = num + 1; // for 1 to 10 

OR :let random = Math.floor( (Math.random() * 10) +1 )
